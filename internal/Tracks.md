# Tracks

The learning tracks drawn as coloured lines on the subway map. Each track is an ordered list of slugs from `dictionary/`; the line visits its stations in this order. A term listed on more than one track becomes an interchange.

Every term must sit on at least one track (`npm run validate` checks this). When you add a term, place it on a track here and give it a map position in `lib/map/stationPlacements.ts`.
