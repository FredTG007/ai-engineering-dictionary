Tokens are the units of text a language model processes. They're often whole words, or pieces of rarer ones. As a rough rule, a token is about four characters of English, but code, numbers and other languages take more.

Almost every limit and cost is measured in tokens: the context window, rate limits, and pricing, where output usually costs several times more than input. One team saw costs triple in a month with no change in traffic. A feature was pasting full JSON responses into the prompt. Trimming the fields and reformatting as compact text cut input tokens by about seventy percent.

Watch output tokens too, since the model writes them one at a time, so longer answers take longer. Ask for concise replies, and cap output length.
