Prompt versioning means giving every prompt a tracked history, with an I D for each version, a record of who changed what, and a way to tell which version produced any output. It treats prompts as deployable artifacts, not strings edited in place.

The pain is usually an unexplained change in behaviour. One team's classification accuracy dropped from ninety-four to eighty-seven percent over a month. The system prompt lived in a database row that three people edited through an admin panel, with no history. Nobody knew what it had said when accuracy was good.

At minimum, attach a version I D to every logged request, keep diffs, store the model and its settings with the prompt, and make rollback instant. Without that, your evals and A-B tests describe a prompt that may no longer exist.
