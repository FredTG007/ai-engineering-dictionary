An embedding is a list of numbers that captures the meaning of a piece of text. Two sentences that mean the same thing, like "how do I reset my password" and "I forgot my login", end up close together, even though they share almost no words.

That closeness is what makes semantic search work. You embed your documents once, store the vectors, then embed each question and look for its nearest neighbours.

The catch is that every vector has to come from the same model. One team upgraded the model for queries but never re-embedded their documents. Nothing crashed, the numbers were still the right length, but search results turned close to random.

Embeddings also struggle with exact strings like error codes, which is why many teams pair them with keyword search.
