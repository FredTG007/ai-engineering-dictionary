Metadata filtering means narrowing a search to documents whose attributes match explicit conditions, like a customer I D or a publication date. Each stored chunk carries a few fields next to its embedding, and the filter turns them into rules similarity alone can't express.

The problem usually shows up as a leak or a stale answer. One internal assistant quoted a pricing sheet from twenty twenty-two, because it was the closest match. Worse, a multi-tenant support bot returned a paragraph from another customer's contract. Similarity search ranks by meaning, not permission or freshness.

So attach the fields you'll need, like source, owner, version and access group, when you ingest. And treat tenant filters as a security boundary: take them from the logged-in session, never from text the model or user produced.
