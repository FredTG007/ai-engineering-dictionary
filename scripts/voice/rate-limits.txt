Rate limits are the ceilings a provider puts on how much you can send in a window of time, usually requests and tokens per minute. Cross one and the call is rejected with a four twenty-nine error.

Most teams discover their limits at launch. One summarization feature worked fine in staging, then failed for about a third of users on the first Monday morning. A nightly batch job shared the same key and had already used most of the token budget.

Token limits usually bite before request limits, because long prompts are expensive. Pace calls with a client-side queue, back off with jitter on rejections, and give batch work its own key. And surface those errors in your dashboards, so a quota problem looks like one.
