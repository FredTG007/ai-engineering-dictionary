Offline evals run your system over a fixed dataset before a change ships. Online evals measure how it behaves on real traffic afterwards. Offline tells you a change is safe to release. Online tells you whether it actually helped.

The gap shows up as a release that passed everything and still hurt. One team's new prompt beat the old one on their golden dataset, shipped, and a week later escalations to human agents were up fifteen percent. Their test set was tidy single-question tickets. Real users pasted long email threads with three questions in them.

So run both and connect them. Gate every change with offline evals, roll out gradually while watching live signals, and score a sample of production traces. Then turn each real failure into a new offline case.
