Time to first token is how long it takes from sending a request until the model produces its first piece of output. It covers network time, queueing, and the model reading your entire prompt.

It's the speed users feel most. A reply that starts quickly and finishes in six seconds often feels faster than one that appears all at once after four. One team watched this creep from under a second to nearly four over a quarter. The system prompt had grown, retrieval had gone from three chunks to twelve, and long chats sent the full history every turn.

The biggest lever is prompt length. Trim retrieval, summarize older turns, and use prompt caching. Then track the ninety-fifth percentile, not just the average.
