Turn detection is how a voice agent decides the person has finished speaking and it's time to reply. Machines have to infer it from audio and partial transcripts.

There are two ways to fail, pulling in opposite directions. Too early, and the agent cuts people off. Too late, and every exchange has dead air. A bank's voice line waited for half a second of silence. Callers reading out card numbers paused naturally between groups of four digits, and the agent kept jumping in to say the number wasn't valid.

Context helps a lot. If you just asked for a card number, wait for sixteen digits. After a yes or no question, a short yes is a complete turn. And when the user talks over the agent, stop speaking quickly.
