Red teaming means trying to break your own AI system on purpose. A red team, whether staff, outside specialists or automated attack generators, attempts jailbreaks, prompt injection, data extraction and tool misuse, then reports what worked.

Its value is what it catches before launch. A bank preparing an account-assistant agent ran a two-week exercise. Testers found that asking it to summarize recent activity, including linked accounts, revealed transactions from a spouse's joint account the user wasn't authorized to see. The bug was in tool permissions, not the model, but only adversarial use exposed it.

Combine human testers, who find creative multi-step attacks, with automated variations for coverage. Turn every successful attack into a regression test. And run another pass after any new tool, model swap or data source.
