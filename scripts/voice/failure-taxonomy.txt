A failure taxonomy is the short list of named ways your system breaks, each with a count of how often it happens. You build it by reading real traces, writing a one line note on what went wrong in each, and then grouping the notes into modes and counting them.

Vague complaints are the symptom that calls for one. One team's assistant was described in every stand-up as just dumb, and the proposed fix was a bigger model at three times the cost. An engineer read a hundred flagged conversations. Forty percent were one mode: a retrieval index a month out of date. Only eight traces looked like reasoning failures.

Write the note first and pick the category later. Fix the biggest bucket, turn each mode into an eval, and rebuild the taxonomy after every significant change.
