The context window is the hard limit on how much a model can see in a single call. It's measured in tokens, and it covers everything: the system prompt, the conversation history, retrieved documents, tool results, and the reply the model is about to write.

Running out of room can fail loudly, with an error, or quietly, which is worse. One team's assistant started ignoring a user's dietary restrictions late in long conversations. The restriction was stated in turn two, and the history trimmer dropped it once the chat grew long enough. No request ever failed.

A bigger window isn't a free fix either. It costs more, slows the first token, and material in the middle gets less attention. Treat the window as a budget, pin key facts, and log what gets dropped.
