BM25 is a ranking function for keyword search. Given a query, it scores each document by how often the query's terms appear in it, how rare those terms are across the whole corpus, and how long the document is. It has been the default in search engines such as Lucene, Elasticsearch and OpenSearch for years, and it remains a strong baseline that is hard to beat on exact-match queries.

Three ideas drive the score. Term frequency rewards documents that mention a query term more often, but with diminishing returns: the parameter k1, typically around 1.2 to 2.0, controls how quickly extra mentions stop helping. Inverse document frequency (IDF) gives rare terms more weight than common ones, so "ERR_4012" counts for far more than "the". Length normalization, controlled by b (usually 0.75), stops long documents from winning simply because they contain more words.

BM25 earns its place in AI systems because [embeddings](./embeddings.md) are weak at exact strings. A support team found their [RAG](./rag.md) assistant could explain billing concepts well but returned unrelated pages whenever a customer pasted an error code or SKU. [Similarity search](./similarity-search.md) treated the code as noise near other codes; BM25 matched it exactly, and the rare identifier's high IDF pushed the right page to the top.

| Strength | Weakness |
|---|---|
| Exact identifiers, names, jargon | Misses paraphrases and synonyms |
| No model or GPU needed | Sensitive to how text is split into terms |
| Fast, cheap, easy to explain | Ignores word order and meaning |
| Works on day one with no training data | Needs per-language analyzers |

The details of how text is split into terms matter more than the formula. An analyzer that breaks "KX-220B" at the hyphen, lowercases it, or applies stemming can quietly destroy the exact matches you wanted. Check how your identifiers are indexed before tuning k1 or b.

In practice BM25 is rarely used alone in LLM applications. It is one half of [hybrid search](./hybrid-search.md), with results merged with vector search and then passed to [reranking](./reranking.md). Measure the combination with [retrieval metrics](./retrieval-metrics.md) on a query set that includes identifiers as well as natural-language questions.

_Usage:_

- "Add a BM25 leg for the part numbers, the embeddings will never get those right."
- "The analyzer was splitting SKUs on hyphens, so BM25 couldn't match them either."
